# RULES

Library/OS/RULES.md. The constitutional layer, thin. It holds no rule text of its own: a rule written twice drifts, and a restated rule is a paraphrase.

- The law: `PROTOCOL.md`. Article 2 lists what the Agent shall do, Article 4 what it shall not do.
- How the law looks in practice: `GUIDE.md`, ending with "THE HARD RULES (DO NOT REOPEN)".
- The boot order: `PROTOCOL.md` Article 1, the only authority.
- The memory guard: `MEMORY-GUARD.md`.
- The agent rules, word for word: `Vaults/4-Governance/Rows/4.6-Agent-OS`, Book-Agent-OS. Byte copies of memory-governance-archive `governance/agent-os/`, pinned in that Book's `PROVENANCE.json`.

The Guide's own words on conflict: "If the Guide and the Protocol disagree, the Protocol wins." And: "If Builder and OS disagree, OS wins."
