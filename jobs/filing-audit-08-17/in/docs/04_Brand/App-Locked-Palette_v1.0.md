## Blue page-colour group — RESEARCH (2026-06-14, arsenic/muting method)

The founder's "arsenic principle": SHADOW = a MUTED tone (not necessarily dark); a minuscule drop of the third primary mutes the whole mix, like one drop of arsenic in 10 L of water. Page colours are built in GROUPS that share one origin (the trio) + one muting → any colour in a group pairs with any other, and with another group built the same way. Cohesion = shared origin, not matching hue.
