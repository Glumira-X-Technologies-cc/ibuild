The colour mixer is the founder's painting method as a live tool: https://claude.ai/artifact/FVtXwLDKBysHYPr83EKCKk
(source D:\claude-wip\colour-mixer). His rulings on it, verbatim where quoted, 2026-10-15:

- "Not Cobalt": the electric violet blue was wrong. Cobalt must be a true Winsor & Newton Cobalt Blue; its tints are
  a clean sky blue, never violet or cyan.
- "ave you ever seen a shadow that is lighter than it's owner": a shadow is never lighter than its light; white never
  pushes a shadow above it.
- "to me the area between green lines is the shadow": the shadow is a narrow, darker band of the same family, blended
  in, not the whole lower bar in another colour.
- "Lemon does not belong here" and "lemon does not belong in shadow": Lemon and More yellow are for gold lights only.
- "I dont want solid horizontal lines it must be blended": no hard lines in any gradient.
- "your gloss adds white - that is not gloss it is diluting color": gloss is a brighter colour of the same family laid
  over the colour (screen), feathered, from the top right ("light from right makes design alive").
- "no periwinkle is your reminder not mine. Stop adding your notes on my ui": no check chips, warnings, recipe lines or
  explanations on his pages. Method checks stay in Claude's own work.
- "remove ground", "GluMira must be Gold", "split screen / mixer left / mood board right".
- 1-1-1, his pick: "1-1-1 means one drop each of Cobalt, Alizarin and Ochre into the shadow. Keep it."

**Why:** he paints; screen shortcuts (white for gloss, RGB fades, a shadow as a second colour) read as wrong to him at
once, and notes on his UI are clutter he did not ask for.

**How to apply:** mix by the method (spectral paint, not RGB), keep shadows below their lights, and show him only his
controls and the hexes. Related: [[feedback-gold-one-metallic]], [[feedback-no-paraphrasing-canon]].
